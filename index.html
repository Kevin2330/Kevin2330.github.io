About Me

A bit more about who I am

I'm a PhD student in Quantitative Marketing at the Fuqua School of Business, Duke University. My research sits at the intersection of platform economics, causal inference, and digital markets.

Before coming to Duke, I earned my MA in Economics from the University of Chicago and my BS in Mathematics & Economics (with a specialization in Computing) from UCLA. When I'm not running regressions or reading papers, you can find me exploring local coffee shops, hiking, or trying to perfect my cooking skills.

I believe the best research comes from genuine curiosity about how the world works — and I try to bring that energy into everything I do.
