Hi, my name is

Kaiwen Dong.

PhD Student in Quantitative Marketing @ Duke

Digital Platforms • Ecommerce • Unstructured Data • Causal Inference

I study how digital platforms design and curate the information consumers see — and the market consequences of those choices. My research investigates AI-generated content as a platform information-design tool, examining when it distorts consumer-facing signals versus when it improves decision-making through personalization. I combine large-scale computational methods — NLP and machine learning — with causal inference and survey experiments to quantify these effects and evaluate their welfare implications for consumers, sellers, and platforms. More broadly, I am interested in how AI-mediated information intermediation reshapes competition, consumer behavior, and the distribution of surplus across platform ecosystems.

About Me

A bit more about who I am

I'm a first-year PhD student in Quantitative Marketing at the Fuqua School of Business, Duke University. My research sits at the intersection of information economics, platform design, and AI in marketing.

Before coming to Duke, I earned my MA in Economics from the University of Chicago (MACSS-ECON) and my BS in Mathematics & Economics (with a specialization in Computing) from UCLA after transferring from UCSD. I attended Saint John's High School in Shrewsbury, MA. I like doing roadtrips, hiking, playing soccer, and making EDM on FL Studio 12.

I believe the best research comes from genuine curiosity about how the world works — and I try to bring that energy into everything I do.
