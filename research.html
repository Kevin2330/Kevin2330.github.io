Research

My work focuses on understanding how digital platforms shape markets, using causal inference and structural methods.

Working Papers

Platform Design and Seller Competition in Digital Marketplaces

with [Coauthor Name], [Coauthor Name]

This paper examines how platform design choices—such as search ranking algorithms and fee structures—affect competition among sellers and consumer welfare in digital marketplaces. Using a structural model of platform interactions combined with quasi-experimental variation in platform policies, we quantify the trade-offs platforms face when optimizing for short-term revenue versus long-term market health. Our findings suggest that policies promoting seller diversity can increase total platform value by 12–18%.

Causal Effects of Algorithmic Recommendations on Consumer Search

with [Coauthor Name]

We study how algorithmic product recommendations causally affect consumer search behavior and purchase decisions. Leveraging a natural experiment from a major e-commerce platform's algorithm update, we identify the causal effect of recommendation quality on search depth, product consideration sets, and final purchase outcomes. Results indicate that improved recommendations reduce search costs but may also narrow the diversity of products consumers discover.

Work in Progress

Dynamic Pricing and Market Thickness on Two-Sided Platforms

with [Coauthor Name]

This project investigates how platforms can use dynamic pricing strategies to manage market thickness—the density of buyers and sellers actively participating at any given time. We develop a dynamic model of platform participation and test it using data from a ride-sharing platform.

The Welfare Effects of Data Portability Regulations

Solo-authored

An empirical investigation of how data portability mandates affect competition, innovation, and consumer welfare in digital markets. Using the EU's data portability provisions as a natural experiment, this project quantifies the effects on platform switching costs and market concentration.
